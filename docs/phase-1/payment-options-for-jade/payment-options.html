How students pay

The plans, the prices, how a student pays today, how each payment is checked, and what is coming next.
9 November 2026 · Prepared by Dr. Ian A. Forde II

In one minute

Three plans: Free, 1 month for TT$200, and 3 months for TT$500. Both paid plans unlock everything.

How students pay today: by bank transfer into NRG's local account. They upload the receipt on the website, and an NRG admin approves it after finding the payment on the bank statement. The plan then starts at once.

Coming later: paying online by card. The website already shows it as "coming soon".

Where it stands: everything is built and tested on the test site. It goes live once NRG's bank details are added and the live site is updated. The last page lists what we need from you.

How time is counted

A plan runs for 1 or 3 calendar months from the day it is approved. Access stops by itself when the time is up, and the student goes back to the free plan.

A student who renews before their plan ends loses nothing: the new time is added after their current end date. Students will also get reminder emails 7 days and 1 day before their plan ends (being added now).

The free plan's limits are switched off on the live site today; they come on when you and Ian decide.

2. Paying by bank transfer AVAILABLE FIRST

This is how students will pay when plans go live. It needs no card company, so it can start as soon as NRG's bank details are on the site.

What the student does

  1. Chooses a plan on the website.
  2. Pays the exact price into NRG's account, by bank transfer, online banking or a cash deposit at the bank. They use their NRG email address as the payment reference.
  3. Fills in a short form: the plan, the date they paid, how they paid, the reference, and a photo or PDF of the receipt.
  4. Sees "We're checking your payment" on their plan page until it is approved.

What NRG does

  1. An admin opens the Payments page, which lists every payment waiting to be checked.
  2. They open the receipt and look for the payment on NRG's bank statement. This is the real check: a receipt alone is never enough.
  3. They tick three boxes (payment found on the statement, amount correct, plan correct) and press Approve. The page shows the date the plan will end before they approve.
  4. If something is wrong, they press Reject and write a reason. The student sees the reason on their plan page and can fix it and send again.

We promise students an answer within 2 working days (please confirm this suits you; see the last page).

Protection against fake or reused receipts

Each receipt is checked automatically the moment it is uploaded. The admin is warned if:

  • the file is not really a photo or PDF;
  • the file carries an AI tool's label (images made with ChatGPT, Gemini and similar tools often say so in the file);
  • the same receipt or the same payment reference was already used by someone else.

A warning adds one more tick before approval: "I found this payment on the bank statement itself." No warning doesn't prove a receipt is real, so the bank statement check always comes first.

3. Other things worth knowing

TopicHow it works
Wrong amount paidThe payment can't be approved for a different amount. The admin rejects it with the reason, and the student pays the difference and sends the receipt again.
RefundsA rejected payment is refunded by bank transfer outside the website; the admin records the refund date and reference on the payment. Proposed: within 10 working days. Please confirm.
One payment at a timeA student can't send a second payment while one is still being checked.
Ending a plan earlyAn admin can end a plan, with a reason (for example, an account being shared). The student sees that it was ended by NRG.
Account sharingEach student account works on one device at a time: signing in on a new device signs the old one out. Admins see a list of accounts that look shared, to review; nothing is banned automatically. This protects your paid content.
PrivacyReceipts are visible only to NRG admins, open through links that expire after 10 minutes, and are deleted 90 days after they are checked.
Staff and giftsTeachers and admins never pay. An admin can still give a student a plan by hand (for a scholarship or a test account).
Record keepingEvery approval, rejection, refund and early ending is logged with who did it and when.

4. Paying online by card COMING SOON

The plans page already shows "Pay online by card: coming soon". It is not built yet, and it does not hold anything up: bank transfer works without it.

What it will take

  • A card payment company that works in Trinidad and Tobago and supports repeat payments. WiPay and PayPal are the options we know of; neither is confirmed yet.
  • Where the money goes: how that company pays out to NRG's account.
  • Prices in US dollars for students outside Trinidad and Tobago: US$29 for 1 month and US$69 for 3 months are stored on the platform, ready for this, and not shown until card payment exists.
  • Business and tax status for a merchant account.

When it is ready, the "coming soon" card becomes a "Pay by card" button and approval happens automatically; bank transfer can stay as a second option.

What we need from you

  • NRG's bank details for students to pay into: bank, branch, account name, account number. (Payments stay closed on the website until these are entered.)
  • Who approves payments: you, Ian, or both.
  • How fast we promise to check a receipt: 2 working days, or ____.
  • Refunds: within 10 working days is fine, or ____.
  • A student or school discount? ____
