Part A. What is already built

Grouped by who uses it. Each entry says what it does in plain terms and where it is.

For students: studying

Practice questions (tutor mode)

LIVE

Students pick a domain, topic, taxonomy level or difficulty and answer questions one at a time. After "Check answer" they see the correct option, an explanation and a rationale for every option, colour-coded as correct, close, priority or incorrect. Answer keys never reach the browser before the student answers.

Keyboard shortcuts (1–9 or A–D to choose, Enter to check and move on) are on staging since 9 Nov.

Flashcards

LIVE

Rapid recall by topic: flip, shuffle and drill.

Mock exams

LIVE

Exam-format papers set by a teacher, taken alone or in a group of up to five, with an optional timer. No feedback while answering; explanations stay hidden until the teacher releases them. Each result includes a fatigue analysis: accuracy and pace by quarter of the exam, and a recommendation when accuracy drops late.

Topic reviews (15)

STAGING

Jade's 15 slide decks turned into readable reviews (normal function, what goes wrong, assessment, management, care plan, how the exam tests it), each with a practice question and a "practise this topic" button. Questions link back to the matching review after they are answered. Reading a review to the end earns 30 XP. The library has a filter panel (search, body system, read or not, sort, card or list view).

All 15 are still drafts: students see none until they are published (Part B).

Case studies

PARTLY BUILT

An unfolding-case player: the patient's story moves through stages with vitals, labs, medications, a calculator and reference ranges; answers lock per stage and feedback comes at the end. Teachers review, validate and publish cases; a generator drafts new ones to Jade's standard.

The player is live; 6 pilot cases wait for Jade's validation, so students have no cases yet.

My progress (private analytics)

STAGING

New on 9 Nov (decision D8, option 2). Each student sees only their own figures, never compared with classmates:

  • Rank, questions answered, accuracy, days studied, domains practised.
  • Strengths and weaknesses by RENR domain (New / Weak / Developing / Mastered, using the ranking rules) and by taxonomy level, each with a "practise" link.
  • Weakest and strongest topics, linked to the matching topic review and to practice on that topic, plus a "focus next" card.
  • Exam stamina from their mock exams, and a 12-week study calendar.

Mock answers count only once the teacher releases that exam's explanations, so the page can't give away which answers were right.

Search and shortcuts

STAGING

Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac) or "/" opens a search box that jumps to any study page, topic review or domain practice.

Nursing Knowledge facts (133)

PARTLY BUILT

A rotating "did you know" block of short clinical facts. Built; waiting for Jade's clinical sign-off before students rely on it.

For students: motivation and rank

XP, ranks and study streak

LIVE

Students earn XP for practice, mock exams and approved questions, and climb Jade's 25-level Benner ladder (Novice to Expert). A daily study streak counts Trinidad days.

Ranking v2 and promotion quizzes

STAGING

Weighted XP (more for weak topics, less for mastered ones), a streak bonus, a mastery milestone, a daily cap, and promotion by a 25-question timed quiz instead of automatic level-ups.

Running in "shadow" on the live site: it calculates quietly next to today's rules so the two can be compared before launch.

Trophies and celebrations

STAGING

56 badges (a rank badge per promotion, tier trophies, and accomplishments such as first mock, streaks and mastered domains), a rank-up ceremony, a trophy case, and a classmates' wall students can opt out of. Launches with ranking v2.

Write a question + AI grading

STAGING

Students write their own question on a topic the platform assigns; an AI grader scores it against Jade's template with feedback per criterion, and teachers see the grade. Writing questions has been live since September; the AI grading, the optional 24-hour rule and the writers' leaderboard are on staging, switched off until Jade chooses (D1–D7).

For students: look and feel

Redesign and four themes

LIVE

The whole app restyled; students choose Royal, Teal, Midnight (dark) or Sunset, each with its own fonts. Switching is instant (a lag fix is on staging since 7 Nov).

Profile, messages and calendar

LIVE

Profile picture and name, private messages with teachers (a student can cite a mock attempt), and an event calendar.

For teachers and admins

Question review queue

LIVE

Teachers approve, edit or send back questions; every AI question needs a named reviewer before it can go live.

Generation requests

LIVE

Jade asks for new questions or cases either by picking domain, topic, taxonomy and difficulty, or by describing what he wants in his own words; the request is queued for the AI author and the results land in his review queue.

Mock exam builder and release

LIVE

Build question sets, set a timer, assign to students or groups, and release explanations when ready. A cohort fatigue card shows whether the class tires late in exams.

Class analytics (at-risk list and class trends)

STAGING

A teacher page listing students at risk and why: no answers for 7 days, under 50% over their last 50 answers, a recent mock below the pass mark, or a 15-point accuracy drop in a fortnight. Each reason shows the student's own numbers, with a one-click message to that student. Below it: 12 weeks of students practising, class accuracy and average mock score, and class accuracy by domain against the month before. The teacher dashboard shows how many students are at risk. Students never see this page.

Thresholds are settings, adjustable without code. Set by Ian on 9 Nov: mock pass mark 66%, a mock counts as recent for 30 days, and accuracy rules wait for 20 answers.

Accounts and access

LIVE

Email sign-up and verification, four roles (student, teacher, admin, super admin), teacher approval, suspending accounts. Admins can set a student's plan by hand.

Content protection

LIVE

A faint watermark with the student's identity on question pages, no copy or print, and blurring when the window loses focus or screenshot keys are pressed. Staff can reveal a watermark from a leaked screenshot. (No website can fully block screenshots; the watermark makes leaks traceable.)

Free plan limits

STAGING

Free students get 10 practice questions a day, no mock exams, and one topic review a day for 5 minutes. Enforced by the database. Built with the switch off; nothing changes for students until it is turned on.

Paid plans by bank transfer

STAGING

Free, 1 month (TT$200) and 3 months (TT$500). Students pay into NRG's account, upload the receipt and see its status; an admin matches it to the bank statement and approves or rejects with a reason. Receipts are checked automatically for AI-made images and reuse. Paid access ends by itself at expiry, and renewing early adds time on. Card payment shows as "coming soon".

Waits for NRG's bank details before students can pay.

One device per student, and shared-account alerts

STAGING

A student account can be signed in on one device at a time: a new sign-in signs the old device out and tells it why. Admins see a list of accounts that keep signing each other out or use many browsers, review them, and adjust the thresholds. Staff are not affected.

Recordings library

STAGING

Past recordings and lectures, like a video journal: filter by type, domain and length; a watch page with chapters, notes, a searchable transcript and captions; it remembers where you stopped and links to the topic review and practice. Paid plans watch everything; free students get samples. Teachers add recordings with a form. Two sample videos are up for review.

Security underneath

LIVE

Row-level security on every table, answer keys hidden from student accounts, and table permissions tightened to only what each role uses; automated tests check all of it.
