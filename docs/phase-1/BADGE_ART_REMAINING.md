# Badge art: what's left to make

**Done (2026-10-13):** all 5 rank medals and the Advanced Beginner trophy, in `public/badges/`.
**Left:** 4 trophies and 27 accomplishment shields. Everything you need is on this page.

## How to work
- Use **Nano Banana (Gemini)**. Do each group in **one chat** so the shapes stay identical.
- Every image: **flat pure white background, no text or numbers**. I remove the white and add any words in the app.
- Save each image into `trophy/` and tell me. I'll cut it out, check it, and add it to the app.
- If something comes back with a shadow, a tinted background, a checkerboard or text, reply in the same chat:
  *"same badge, background pure flat white, no shadow, no text"*.

## Part 2: the 27 accomplishment shields
**Start a new chat** (shields are a different shape from the trophies). Choose one way:

### Way A: three sheets of nine (3 prompts total)
Each prompt draws nine shields in a grid; I split the sheet into separate badges. Paste the **sheet style block** at the
end of each prompt.

**Sheet style block**
> Every badge fully isolated with wide empty white space between badges and around the edges, no badge touching or
> overlapping another, all badges the same size and drawn in one consistent style: polished semi-flat vector illustration
> with soft gradients, a gentle top-left highlight and a crisp dark outline around each badge's outer edge, on a solid flat
> pure white background (#FFFFFF), no drop shadows, no glow, no sparkles, no floor, no gradient or texture in the background,
> no checkerboard pattern, no grid lines, no text, no letters, no numbers, no labels or captions, no watermark, square 1:1.
